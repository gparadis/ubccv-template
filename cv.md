## 13. OTHER RELEVANT INFORMATION (Maximum One Page)

**Research partnerships**: I work with federal and provincial monitoring agencies, and with non-profit conservation organizations, to translate environmental data into defensible statistical evidence. These partnerships shape both my methods research and the training my students receive.

**Open scholarship**: All software and course materials I develop are released under open licenses. I maintain the [rexample](https://example.org/rexample) package, which implements several of the methods from my research in a form that practitioners can apply directly.

In summary, my program combines methodological research, open software, and sustained partnerships with data-using organizations. This mix keeps the research grounded in real problems and gives students experience with the full arc from question to reproducible answer.
